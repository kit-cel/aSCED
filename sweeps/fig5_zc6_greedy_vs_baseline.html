Extends Fig. 5 of the paper (5G LDPC codes C5G(78,60) and C5G(180,60), Zc=6, RL structure) with a greedy RL/QC-block selection in place of "subsequent blocks." Circles = original selection, triangles = greedy selection. Click a legend pill to toggle one series, or use the buttons to compare the two wholesale.

Both codes share one axis (OSD-2 reference for n=78 is near 5.0dB; OSD-4 for n=180 near 2.7dB, per the paper's Fig. 5). The classifier bug described in sweeps/STATUS.md (silent mislabeling of "..._subsplit1" variants, not just data loss — verified via 4 independent re-runs) has been corrected here; a separate, unrelated 0.6dB outlier in the original n=180 reference data (Δ=3 @ 64 paths) was excluded pending its own investigation.
