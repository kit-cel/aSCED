BCH(63,18) affine-subcode selections used in the final 500-error FER figure

Pool indexing:
  matrix_XXX.npz corresponds to pool index XXX in the expanded 52-matrix pool.

ASCED-8 / Subcode ensemble-8:
  Four subcode matrices, each decoded in its subcode and affine-coset branch,
  giving eight decoder arms.
  Selected pool indices: [5, 6, 12, 15]

ASCED-16 / Subcode ensemble-16:
  Eight subcode matrices, each decoded in its subcode and affine-coset branch,
  giving sixteen decoder arms.
  Selected pool indices: [5, 6, 12, 15, 4, 13, 14, 16]

The ASCED-16 matrix selection is nested: its first four matrices are ASCED-8.

Each individual NPZ contains the SSPCM, punctured columns, affine
representative, and appended check. The JSON sidecar gives the appended-check
support and weight and the exact per-block s and t values.
